Movies & Shows

Three Favourites — and Why

One film and two series I keep coming back to — a crime thriller, a Regency romance and a school comedy-drama about growing up.

#1
2021 · CRIME THRILLER

Nemesis

An underworld kingpin, John Morgan, returns to London from semi-retirement, and old scores, family feuds and betrayal catch up with him fast. I love the tension, the London setting and the way loyalty and revenge keep colliding.

#2
2020 – PRESENT · PERIOD ROMANCE / DRAMA

Bridgerton

Regency London, high society, balls, gossip and the Bridgerton family searching for love. The costumes, the music and the drama of each season make it easy to watch one episode after another.

#3
2020 – 2023 · COMEDY-DRAMA

Never Have I Ever

Devi Vishwakumar, a 15-year-old Indian-American student, tries to reinvent herself at school after losing her father. Funny, honest and relatable — about friendship, family, grief and growing up.
